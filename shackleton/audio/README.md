# Audio for the Shackleton exhibit

Put recordings here and run `python rename_shackleton_audio.py --apply --write-config` (it asks which moods each track suits and writes the list into `../index.html`). Moods the exhibit reports: **voyage, ice, peril, grief, triumph**.

Suggestions that suit the period and the mood, all free to use:

- **Banjo, as Hussey played it on the ice.** Recordings published before 1923 are public domain in the United States (since 2022). Fred Van Eps recorded hundreds of banjo sides for Victor and Columbia from 1910 to 1920; the Internet Archive's Great 78 Project has many as downloadable MP3s (search "Van Eps" at https://archive.org/details/georgeblood), and the Library of Congress National Jukebox lists them (e.g. https://www.loc.gov/item/jukebox-12184/). A quiet rag under the camp scenes is exactly what the men heard.
- **Period songs** from the same source: "Keep the Home Fires Burning" (1914) and the hymns sung at the funerals of the Ross Sea party men were all recorded before 1923.
- **Scott Buckley's cinematic library** (CC BY 4.0, credit required; the credit line under the Music button does this): https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/mood/emotional/ and https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/mood/intense/ — solo strings for the ice and the deaths, slow builds for the perils and the wave.
- **Sea and wind ambience:** a CC0 loop from freesound.org (search "antarctic wind" or "blizzard"), listed under `sfx.ambience`.

Licences: check each recording's page before publishing. Pre-1923 recordings are public domain in the US but may not be everywhere.
